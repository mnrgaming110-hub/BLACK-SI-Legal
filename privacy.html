Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

BLACK SI Media operates BLACK SI, a creator-managed video distribution workflow. This notice describes the intended operation of the application. The operator must update this notice if actual data practices differ.

Information processed

When authorized accounts are connected, BLACK SI may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, video files, video IDs, captions, publishing status and technical logs. BLACK SI does not require connected-platform passwords.

Purposes

Information is used to detect new videos, prevent duplicate publishing, transfer media to connected services, publish to authorized accounts, and troubleshoot failures. BLACK SI does not sell personal information.

Third-party services

Depending on enabled integrations, videos and associated metadata may be processed by YouTube, Cloudinary, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Pinterest and Threads. Their privacy terms apply independently. Published videos may be publicly visible.

Storage and protection

Operational credentials should be kept in access-controlled secrets storage. Video files, identifiers and logs may be retained as needed for operations and removed when no longer necessary, subject to service limitations. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Revocation and deletion

Account owners can revoke BLACK SI access through the connected platform's settings. To request access to or deletion of information controlled by BLACK SI, contact the operator via the project owner profile. Revoking access does not automatically delete content already published to third-party platforms.

Children

BLACK SI is not intended for children and does not knowingly seek children's personal information.

Updates

This policy may be updated as integrations change. The current version will be posted here.

Contact

Contact the operator via the GitHub owner profile. A dedicated privacy contact method should be provided before broad public distribution.
